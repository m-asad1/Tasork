## 2. Final Name
**Tasork**

Rationale: Short, brandable, easy to pronounce globally, domain-friendly, and doesn't box the company into "tasks" or "freelance" connotations once paired with the "Solutions" brand language.

## 3. Tagline
**"Describe it. We'll solve it."**

Alternates kept on file for A/B testing:
- "Your Project. Our Solution."
- "Solutions, Delivered Professionally."

## 4. Short Description
Tasork is a SaaS platform where clients submit digital projects and receive a custom-reviewed solution — professionally scoped, priced, and delivered by a managed team of experts.

## 5. Long Description
Tasork replaces the friction of freelance marketplaces with a single, professional workflow: clients describe what they need, upload their requirements, and submit a request. Tasork's internal team manually reviews every submission, evaluates feasibility, and returns a custom Solution Proposal covering price, timeline, deliverables, and terms. Once accepted and paid, the project is assigned internally and executed under agency-level quality control, with the client tracking progress through a dedicated dashboard from request to final delivery.

## 6. Mission
To simplify how digital work gets done by replacing freelancer searching and negotiation with a single, trusted request-to-solution workflow.

## 7. Vision
To become one of the world's most trusted platforms for getting digital projects solved — reliably, transparently, and at professional quality — for anyone, anywhere.

## 8. Core Values
- **Trust first** — every commitment made is a commitment kept.
- **Transparency** — clear pricing, clear timelines, clear communication, no hidden fees.
- **Craftsmanship** — every Solution is reviewed for quality before it reaches the client.
- **Respect for time** — minimal friction, fast responses, no unnecessary back-and-forth.
- **Confidentiality** — client data and project details are protected by default.
- **Accountability** — Tasork owns the outcome, not just the introduction.

## 9. Brand Personality
Premium, calm, competent, and quietly confident — the tone of a senior consultant, not a gig marketplace. Modern but not flashy; professional but not corporate/stiff. Think "boutique agency energy delivered at SaaS scale."

**Voice traits:** clear, reassuring, precise, solution-oriented, never salesy.

## 11. Competitors
- **Upwork / Fiverr** — open marketplaces, client picks and negotiates with individual freelancers directly (high effort, variable quality).
- **Toptal** — curated freelancer matching, still freelancer-facing rather than agency-managed.
- **Local dev/design agencies** — high trust but slow, expensive, and not self-serve or SaaS-based.
- **DesignJoy / Draft.dev (productized service model)** — closest philosophical match (submit → managed delivery), but usually narrow in scope (one service category only).

Tasork's differentiation: broad service scope + productized SaaS workflow + agency-level quality control, in one platform.

## 12. Unique Selling Proposition (USP)
**"You never search for a freelancer. You submit a project, and Tasork solves it."**
One request, one point of contact, one accountable team — instead of a marketplace of unknowns.

## 13. Elevator Pitch
"Most platforms make you search, compare, and negotiate with strangers to get work done. Tasork flips that: you describe your project once, our team reviews it, and you get back a clear proposal — price, timeline, and deliverables — from a single accountable team. Accept it, pay securely, and track your Solution from start to finish."

## 14. Future Vision (5 Years)
- Recognized as a leading agency-model SaaS platform for digital services across multiple regions and languages.
- AI-assisted intake that pre-scopes and pre-prices simple requests in minutes, with human review for complex ones.
- Dedicated in-house expert pods per category (dev, design, writing, data, marketing).
- Enterprise accounts with dedicated account managers and SLAs.
- Subscription-based "Solution Plans" for recurring business needs (e.g., monthly design/dev retainers).
- Public API for partners and agencies to submit and manage projects programmatically.
- A trusted brand where "just Tasork it" becomes shorthand for getting digital work done reliably.
