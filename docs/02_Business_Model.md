# 02 — Business Model

## 1. Business Flow
```
Client submits a Solution Request
        ↓
Internal review team evaluates feasibility
        ↓
Request is Approved or Declined
        ↓
If approved → Solution Proposal is drafted
  (price, timeline, deliverables, payment plan, revision policy)
        ↓
Client reviews and Accepts / Requests changes / Declines
        ↓
Advance payment collected
        ↓
Project assigned internally to the right expert/team
        ↓
Execution with milestone-based progress updates
        ↓
Internal QA review before delivery
        ↓
Delivery to client
        ↓
Revision window (per policy)
        ↓
Final payment (if milestone-based)
        ↓
Project marked Completed
        ↓
Client leaves a review
```

## 2. Customer Journey
1. **Discovery** — lands on marketing site via search, referral, or ads.
2. **Evaluation** — reads how it works, pricing philosophy, testimonials, FAQ.
3. **Submission** — creates an account (or submits as guest lead), fills out the Solution Request form, uploads files.
4. **Waiting** — receives confirmation and estimated review time (e.g., 24–48h).
5. **Proposal** — receives Solution Proposal in-dashboard + email notification.
6. **Decision** — accepts, requests clarification, or declines.
7. **Payment** — pays deposit/advance via supported gateway.
8. **Execution** — tracks progress, messages the team, receives milestone updates.
9. **Delivery** — reviews deliverables, requests revisions if within scope.
10. **Closure** — pays remaining balance (if any), project closes, leaves a review.
11. **Retention** — receives future offers, referral incentives, repeat submissions.

## 3. Revenue Sources
- **Per-project fees** — the core revenue stream; price set per custom proposal.
- **Rush/priority fees** — surcharge for expedited turnaround.
- **Revision fees** — beyond the included revision allowance.
- **Add-on services** — e.g., extra source files, extended support, hosting setup.
- **Future: Subscription/retainer plans** — recurring monthly Solution capacity for businesses.
- **Future: Enterprise contracts** — dedicated account management, SLAs, bulk project bundles.
- **Future: Marketplace commission** — if a vetted-partner expert network is introduced.

## 4. Pricing Strategy
- **No public fixed-price catalog** — every project is custom-quoted to protect margin and quality accuracy, consistent with the "no automatic pricing" principle.
- Internal pricing is guided by an **effort-estimation rubric** (complexity tiers × estimated hours × role rate + margin) to keep quotes consistent across reviewers.
- Indicative "starting from" price ranges may be shown per category on the marketing site for expectation-setting only — never binding.
- Transparent breakdown always shown in the proposal: base cost, any rush fee, taxes/fees, total.

## 5. Payment Policy
- Standard: **percentage-based milestone payments** — e.g., 50% advance to start, 50% on delivery (configurable per proposal for larger projects into 3+ milestones).
- Small projects (below a configurable threshold) may require **100% upfront**.
- Accepted methods: credit/debit card and digital wallets via Stripe; local payment rails added per region as the platform expands.
- All payments processed through the platform — no off-platform payment is permitted, for security and dispute protection.
- Funds for in-progress work are tracked per project in the client's Payments/Invoices section.

## 6. Revision Policy
- Each Solution Proposal states a **fixed number of included revision rounds** (e.g., 2 rounds), defined per project type.
- Revisions must fall within the original agreed scope; out-of-scope requests are quoted separately as a change order.
- Revision requests must be submitted within the **revision window** (e.g., 7 days after delivery) via the project's messaging thread.

## 7. Refund Policy
- **Before work starts:** full refund of advance if the client cancels prior to any work being assigned/started.
- **After work starts:** partial refund based on percentage of work completed, assessed by the project manager, minus non-recoverable costs (e.g., third-party licenses already purchased).
- **Non-delivery by Tasork:** full refund if Tasork fails to deliver per the agreed proposal terms.
- **No refunds** after final delivery and acceptance, outside of the revision window.
- All refund decisions are logged with justification for auditability.

## 8. Cancellation Policy
- Clients may request cancellation any time via the dashboard; refund amount follows the Refund Policy above based on project stage.
- Tasork reserves the right to decline or cancel a project (e.g., infeasible, unethical, or policy-violating requests), with a full refund of any uncommenced work.
- Repeated late-stage cancellations may affect a client's account standing.

## 9. Support Policy
- **Channels:** in-dashboard messaging (primary), live chat, and email.
- **Response SLA:** first response within 24 business hours (target 4–8h during business hours); faster for active/priority projects.
- **Scope:** account issues, payment issues, project status questions, dispute escalation.
- **Escalation path:** Support → Project Manager → Operations Lead → Founder/Admin (for unresolved disputes).

## 10. Confidentiality Policy
- All uploaded files and project details are treated as confidential by default.
- Internal access to client projects is limited to assigned staff (role-based access control).
- Optional NDA available on request for sensitive/enterprise projects.
- Client data is never shared with third parties outside of the assigned internal team and required payment/processing providers.

## 11. Risk Management
- **Scope creep risk** → mitigated via explicit proposal scope + paid change orders.
- **Non-payment risk** → mitigated via milestone-based payments collected in advance.
- **Quality risk** → mitigated via mandatory internal QA review before delivery.
- **Unethical/illegal project risk** → mitigated via manual review + acceptance criteria (see Platform Principles).
- **Delivery delay risk** → mitigated via internal capacity planning and buffer time built into estimates.
- **Data loss risk** → mitigated via redundant cloud storage and backups.

## 12. Quality Assurance
- Every deliverable passes through an internal **QA checklist** before being sent to the client (functional check, requirement match, formatting/polish check).
- Random sampling of completed projects reviewed by an Ops Lead for consistency.
- Client satisfaction ratings tracked per project and per internal team member.

## 13. Dispute Resolution
1. Client raises a concern via the project thread or Support.
2. Assigned Project Manager attempts resolution within 48 hours.
3. If unresolved, escalates to Operations Lead for a binding internal review.
4. Resolution options: revision, partial refund, full refund, or continuation with adjusted scope.
5. All disputes and outcomes are logged for quality tracking.

## 14. Referral System
- Clients receive a unique referral link/code from their dashboard.
- Referrer earns a **credit** (e.g., a percentage of the referred client's first project value) once the referred project is paid and completed.
- Referred client may receive a **first-project discount** as an incentive.
- Referral credits are usable toward future Tasork projects (not cash-withdrawable, to preserve platform value).

## 15. Coupon Strategy
- **Welcome coupon** — first-time submitters get a small percentage/flat discount.
- **Seasonal campaigns** — limited-time discounts tied to academic terms, fiscal quarters, etc.
- **Loyalty coupons** — issued after N completed projects.
- **Partner/affiliate codes** — for influencer or B2B partnership channels.
- All coupons configurable in Admin (percentage/flat, expiry, usage limits, category restrictions).

## 16. Future Subscription Plans
- **Starter Retainer** — fixed monthly hours/capacity for small ongoing needs (e.g., content, minor dev tasks).
- **Growth Retainer** — higher monthly capacity with priority queue placement.
- **Enterprise Plan** — dedicated account manager, custom SLA, volume pricing, quarterly reviews.
- Subscriptions convert unused-hour policies (rollover vs. use-it-or-lose-it) to be defined at launch of this feature.
